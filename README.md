# Belly-Talk
BellyTalk is a digital health platform improving access to maternal health services, professional support, trusted health information, and maternity-related products for women in Ethiopia.
Problem
Maternal healthcare in Ethiopia faces several challenges:
Approximately 70% of maternity products are sold through fragmented markets and small boutiques.
88% of pregnant women do not receive quality antenatal care.
36.4% of pregnant women report depression or psychological distress.
These gaps result in limited access to trusted information, healthcare professionals, quality maternity products, and emotional support during pregnancy.
Why Now?
Several factors make this the right time for BellyTalk:
Rapid growth in smartphone adoption.
Ethiopia's Digital Ethiopia 2030 agenda.
Large and highly active online communities of mothers.
Growing demand for accessible digital healthcare solutions.
Our Solution
BellyTalk provides an integrated maternal health ecosystem through:
Online Consultations
Access to healthcare specialists through affordable on-demand consultations.
Hospital Discovery
Helping mothers identify hospitals and connect with healthcare providers.
Professional Peer Group Care
Moderated support communities guided by health professionals.
Education and Trusted Products
Evidence-based educational resources and access to verified maternity vendors.
